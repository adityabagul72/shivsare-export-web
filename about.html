About us

About Our Company

SHIVSAR EXPORT is an India-based commodity trading company with offices in Nashik, India. We are tied up with major brands and have had a presence in the Indian market for the last 24 years. As of 2024, we have started exporting agricultural products worldwide.

We have direct supplier relationships with farmers and factories in India, enabling us to supply products at the most competitive prices. Our company operates from major ports in India, including JNPT Mumbai, Mundra, Chennai, Tuticorin, and all major airports.

SHIVSAR EXPORT ensures top-quality products, timely delivery, and customer satisfaction. We aim to be the preferred destination for agro-product buyers. Our company enjoys an outstanding reputation due to the dedication of our employees and the excellence of our products and services.

All our products meet industry standards and are securely packed per client specifications. Our team’s professional approach enables us to operate smoothly and efficiently. The primary products we supply, export, and trade include:

Red Onion, Pink Onion, White Onion, Nashik Onion, Garwa Onion, Summer Onion, Fresh Onion, and Organic Onion.

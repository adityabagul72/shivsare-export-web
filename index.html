About us

About Our Company

RGH Global is the fastest-growing export company. It is one of the foremost wholesalers and exporters of Nasik dealing with high-quality Indian red onions. For over 48 years, RGH (Rambilas Gulabchand Heda) has been built on the vision of uncompromising quality with the prime objective of trading in agro-based onions. RGH Global is the sister company of RGH which was established in 2019. RGH was headed by Mr. Rambillas Gulabchand Heda from 1972 – 1995, and currently, headed by Mr. Pravin Heda who has experience in the domestic & international market for over 25 years. Mohit Heda is the founder of RGH Global who aims to build a strong brand to provide reliable services for people today and generations to come. Over the years, we’ve built long-standing relationships with our production partners, each one rooted in trust and a commitment to excellence. Now RGH is going one step ahead and getting into the international market under the name RGH Global.
